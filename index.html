Create

Any question bank. Pasted in seconds.

Paste questions straight from your notes, a PDF or an AI assistant. Testmond reads multiple choice and fill‑in‑the‑blank questions — even mixed in one test — and shows you exactly what it found before anything is saved.

Two paste formats

Questions and answers together, or questions in one box and the answer key in another — matched by number.

Preview before you save

See how many questions were found and exactly which were skipped and why.

Edit anything, anytime

Change text, options and answers, reorder questions, delete or add more — one at a time or in bulk.

Pictures on questions

Attach an image to any question. It's compressed and stored inside the test.

Practise

Practise your way.

Pick an answer, submit it, move on. Leave halfway and Testmond remembers your place — including an answer you hadn't submitted yet.

  • Quiz mode shows right or wrong the moment you submit.
  • Practice mode saves the verdict for the end.
  • A stopwatch for every question. It starts when a question opens and stops when you submit it — the review shows how long each one took.
  • Attempt history for every test, with dates and scores.
  • Tap any question in the review to see it in full, with the correct answer, your time and its solution.

Exam Mode

No hints. No pauses.
Just you and the clock.

Pick any test, set the time, and start. It looks like a normal test — except the countdown replaces the title, and nothing tells you what's right until you finish.

59:42
  • Auto‑submit. When the timer hits zero, your exam is submitted for you.
  • Change your mind. There's no Submit button — answers are saved as you pick them and stay editable until you finish.
  • Nothing revealed. No colours, no solutions, no Reset. Leaving asks first, because progress can't be resumed.
  • Every attempt saved. Reopen the review any time to see what you got right and wrong.

Custom marking. Set your own marks for correct, wrong and skipped answers — like +4, −1 and 0. The score in every review, past attempts included, is worked out with your rules.

Solutions

Explanations, with pictures.

Add a solution to any question — text, math and an image. It appears right after you answer and again in every review. Tap the magnifier on any picture to open it full screen: pinch to zoom, drag to look around, double‑tap to zoom in or out.

Math & tables

Formulas that look like formulas.

Testmond has a full LaTeX renderer built in — matrices, fractions, chemistry and units — and it works with no connection at all. Wide formulas shrink to fit the screen, and Markdown tables become real tables that wrap to your display.

Organise & share

Keep it tidy. Send it anywhere.

Group tests in folders. Share a single test — or a whole folder — as one small file over any app. When someone opens it, Testmond shows how many questions each test has and how many tests are inside before anything is imported.

Built for AI

Let an assistant write
the questions.

Testmond includes a format guide you can hand to ChatGPT, Claude or any other assistant. Ask for "20 questions on photosynthesis for Testmond" — or have it clean up a messy question bank — and paste the result straight in.

Privacy

What's on your phone
stays on your phone.

Testmond has no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet access at all. Your questions, answers and scores are stored only on your device.
